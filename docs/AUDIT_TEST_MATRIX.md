## A-03 candidate scenarios pending product decisions

After scope is decided, use fixtures with obligations inside and outside the selected month; payments received in a different month from the obligation settled; partially paid historic arrears; advance/unallocated credit; checked-out tenants; and held/settled deposits. Name each expected measure separately: billed obligations, cash received, outstanding, overdue, advance credit, and deposit liability. Do not assign expected totals before the product decisions are recorded.
